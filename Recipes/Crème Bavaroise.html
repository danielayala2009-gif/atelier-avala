Crème Bavaroise

CRÈME

Ingredients

250ml milk

60g egg yolks (3 yolks)

62.5g sugar

4 gelatine leaves (8g)

1/3 vanilla pod

250ml cream

Directions

1. Add gelatine to cold water in order to make it bloom, needs to be soft (separate leaves to ensure bloomed fully)
2. Open vanilla pod and scrape out seeds, add to milk in saucepan and cook over medium heat
3. Whisk yolks and sugar together, just incorporate both together. Don’t over mix and pit too much air in mixture
4. Once the first bubbles appear (if too hot will burn the yolks) place half the milk mixture onto eggs (tempering the eggs) and whisk to incorporate
5. Place back into saucepan (induction off) use whisk to incorporate then use spatula to stir (calmly, don’t want to add air to mixture)
6. Cook slowly (heat level 2) and continue until 80-82C
7. You will know it is ready when:
1. There is no longer foam (only if you are not stirring like a crazy person)
2. When stopping stirring the mixture will not continue to circle.
3. Cuisse à la nappe - when using a wooden spoon, you can pull it out of the mixture, and make a horizontal line and the top will not run into the bottom
8. Squeeze out gelatine and add to saucepan and stir to dissolve as soon as crème anglaise is ready. Mix well because we aren’t bring this one to the boil again. Remove from saucepan to stop cooking.
9. Whip cream - needs to be cold
10. Assemble cake before adding whipped cream to crème anglaise mixture
11. Remove vanilla pod and add whipped cream once mixture is approx. 40C
