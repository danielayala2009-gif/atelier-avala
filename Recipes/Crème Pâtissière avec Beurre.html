Crème Pâtissière avec Beurre

CRÈME

Ingredients

250g Milk

2 yolks

50g sugar

25g cornstarch

1 gelatine sheet

3g vanilla

185g butter

Directions

1. Place gelatine in water to bloom.
2. Place milk with vanilla in a large saucepan, and cook over medium heat until boils
3. Whisk yolks, sugar and starch (mix together first and make sure there is no lumps) together in a bowl. Mix well.
4. Place half the milk mixture onto eggs (tempering the eggs) and whisk to incorporate right away
5. Pour the egg mixture into the rest of the hot milk, stirring while doing this.
6. Return to heat (heat level 4) to cook, while whisking.
7. For each 1 litre of milk cook for 1 minute after boil.
8. Add gelatine, and mix well
9. Add mixture on top of butter in storage container. Using handheld mixer, mix until butter is fully incorporated.
10. Place in plastic wrap and place in fridge to cool down really fast.
11. 3 days maximum in fridge (Can’t be frozen)
