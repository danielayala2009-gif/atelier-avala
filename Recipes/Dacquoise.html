Dacquoise

BISCUITS

Ingredients

190g almond powder

190g icing sugar

40g flour

6 egg whites

75g sugar

15g hazelnut powder

15g icing sugar

Directions

1. Use piping bag with Tip 806
2. Prepare baking sheet with mould size circle, will be piping in a circle
3. Start whisking Eggs whites, once really foamy add half sugar (once started can’t stop or eggs will separate and deflate)
4. When it becomes really shiny add remaining sugar and whisk until stiff peaks are formed
5. Fold in icing sugar, then flour mixture - this needs to be done quickly but gently or you will deflate the mixture
6. Pipe vertical in a spiral, starting in the middle. Let it fall, we are only guiding it. If you leave space between piping tip and baking sheet you can see where you are going.
7. Sprinkle hazelnuts powder and icing sugar on top of biscuits
8. At end, stop piping and cut with bottom of piping tip
9. Bake @ 370F -+ 10 minutes (set timer for 5 minutes to check) we don’t want it too dark
10. Will last 2-3 days maximum in airtight container. Will freeze well.
