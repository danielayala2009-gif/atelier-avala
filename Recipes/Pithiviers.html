Assembly
1) Divide into 2 even pieces
2) Roll - not to thin, or layers will be smashed
3) Make Ball - take corner and press into middle, then moving around the edge, pressing all corners into the middle. To make the key
4) Turn over so key is facing down, with no flour on table, only on hands. Roll around, using pinkies to press underneath the ball. If there is an air bubble at the top, more rolling is required.
5) Flatten to refrigerate.
6) Once cold, roll out until 3mm thick. Roll back and forth then turn pâte. Don’t go to edge of pâte or edges will not rise. Trying to keep as round as possible.
7) Put in freezer until cold.
8) Cut into round shape (20cm) then make holes using fork
9) Put prettiest round on bottom (on flat parchment paper) with key facing up.
10) Egg wash edge, then pipe crème d’amande inside using Tip-804, in a spiral.
11) Fold other round in half, with key facing up. Place over first round, unfold and adjust size on top round. Remove all air inside, then press around edge of crème d’amande to stop it from spilling.
12) Technique Chiquetter: Press fingers down onto joining edge, then with the back of a small knife, on a 45 degree angle, press to meet fingers (creates rope-like look)
13) Place in freezer until you can draw on pâte
14) Turn so flat side is up. Egg wash two times, then Rayer - draw a pattern starting in the middle, a wave to the edge. Repeat 8 times. Continue going between the lines to split it up even more.
15) Add opposite lines on edge.
16) Make holds on lines as eggs in creme d’amande are going to rise
17) Bake at 350F until Golden Brown Delicious (Approx
