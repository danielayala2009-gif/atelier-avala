Le Chocolat Exotique (58)

Description

1. Imbibage/sirop
65g eau
60g sucre
30g mangue puree
20g passion purèe
20g rhum

2. Crimeux exotique (p 14—15, 4month)
115g passion puree
75g sucre
30g yolks/jaunes
115g oeuf
1.5 pcs gelatine
60g buerre

3. Dacquoise (1/2 recette)

4. Mousse chocolate
Freezer/ congelateur

5. Glaçage/glaze
Chocolate cakea
Assembly



M Mousse M
O Daq O

S Cremeux S

E Daq E

Directions

1. For the syrop put everything together and boil put in container and set aside

2. Crimeux exotique( like lemon curd)
Heat passion fruit
Mix egg yolks and yolks together and add sugar to the egg mix and mix right away- temper with passion fruit -add the tempered eggs to the remaining passion purèe fruit in pan and boil until it thickens -then add bloomed gelatin -mix well then add it to the butter and mix together with the mixer -then pour on a pan with plastic wrap flatten and set in the fridge to cool.

3. Dacquoise (804 pipping tip)
Prepare your circles in the parchment paper and place in a sprayed tray with circles facing down
Mix almond flour, icing sugar and flour together
Beat your egg whites to foam then add your sugar and beat to soft peaks-add flour mix and fold inside-do not over fold
Pipe the dacquoise mix on to the pan

Bake at 375 to prevent the biscuit to be moist and prevent it from over drying

4. Mousse chocolat
Put acetate on the inside of ring in which you will assemble cake
Double boiler melt chocolate then set aside
Whip your cream put in the fridge until ready to use
Sugar and water to 121 degrees (like pate bombe)
Whisk eggs
add the melted sugar to whisked eggs
Then whisk until cooled
In double boiler melt chocolate again if it is too thick
When pate a bombe is cool enough add chocolate and mix with whisk to fully incorporate
Then incorporate cream cold inside the warmer chocolate mix( always cold into hot)

Assembly
Place cremeux inside the pastry bag


Place first Dacquoise inside ring upside down then add sirop
Allow the syrop to soak
Put mouse inside
Chemise mold (cake must be inside mousse-spread to edges
Pipe cremoux and pipe like the Dacquoise
Cover with mousse
Then place the next :, layer
