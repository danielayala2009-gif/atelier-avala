Cake Marbré

ENTREMETS ET GÂTEAUX DE VOYAGE

Ingredients

Vanilla Mix

475g eggs (approx. 50g per large egg)

650g sugar

350g 35% cream

10g baking powder

8g vanilla extract

155g butter

500g flour


Chocolate Mix

275g eggs (approx. 50g per large egg)

375g sugar

215g 35% cream

6g baking powder

60g cocoa powder

95g butter, melted

250g flour

Directions

Vanilla Mix
1) Make butter pommade then add sugar and mix
2) Once incorporated add first egg, and incorporate. Repeat with remaining eggs
3) Add dry ingredients and fold to incorporate.
4) Add cream with vanilla and incorporate.


Chocolate Mix
1) Make butter pommade then add sugar and mix
2) Once incorporated add first egg, and incorporate. Repeat with remaining eggs
3) Add dry ingredients and fold to incorporate.
4) Add cream and incorporate.


Assembly
1) Put vanilla mixture inside oiled mould.
2) Put chocolate mixture in piping bag, and make two lines in the vanilla mixture
3) With a cake knife, turn through the mixtures to make a spiral effect
4) Bake at 330F until ‘Golden Brown Delicious’ (Approx. 30 minutes)
