24/07 Forte Choco noisette (9)

Ingredients

pate sucre chocolat

180 g farine

20g poudre de cocoa

70g icing sugar/ sucre glace

100g beurre

3 yolks/jeune

3g sel


2. appareil a la noisette (1/4) (hazelnut preparation/mix) (financier)


3. cremeux lacté (1/2)-(hazelnut paste)



bake at 330F

Description

Lait concentré
90g lait en poudre
170g créme
140g sucre
40g eau
Boil/bouillir eu et creme
Milanger/mix
Milk, lait et sucre

Directions

1. Pâte sucre chocolat

Combine flour, cocoa powder, icing sugar and salt and sablée with butter
Form a hole in the middle of the sablée mix and fraiser with back of the hand put in the fridge to rest (20min-40min)

2. Appareil a la noisette (1/4) (hazelnut preparation/mix)

*Melt butter

Combine everything hazel nuts, icing sugar then add the and mix with the spatula ensure it is fully incorporated before adding the butter egg whites( butter must not be cold same thing for madeleines and financiers to prevent lumps)

--Roll out dough, roll with pin to make holes and place in the appropriate tart pan--

Pour the appareil noisette inside the tart and bake at 330F

3. Crémeux lacté

Heat cream--Combine egg yolks, hazelnut paste, milk chocolate , and dark chocolate add milk to the side that is not next to your yolks (refrigerate inside the tart)
