Tarte Croustillante Au Chocolat Aux Framboise-crispy Chocolate And Radpberry Cake

Ingredients

1. Biscuit chocolat sans farine


2. Coulis framboise 50g


Sucre 50g

Feille gelatin (gelatin leaf)


Pate sucrée:


180g farine

20 g of cocoa

70g icing- sucre glace

100g buerre

3 yolks

3 g sel


3. Ganache chocolat framboise


4. Croustillant:


75 sucre

10 lait

20g glucose

20g beurre

25g amande

30g cocoa

Directions

Pate sucrée:
Separate yolks
Mix flour, cocoa powder and icing sugar together
Sable butter and flour mix together (make sure there no lumps)then in center add yolk and fraiser with back of hand going forward to mix together thoroughly-do not over-mix- flatten the cover in plastic and allow it to sit in fridge (30min)

2. Raspberry coulis
Add framboise to sugar then add the gelatin(bloom the gelatin in cold water first), boil then add gelatin.
Pour over plastic wrap, flatten and cool.

3. Ganache warm cream, raspberry puree and glucose- heat to boil add chocolate noir and butter- pour hot liquid on top chocolat- start at the middle incorporate the middle then the outer areas
T
Croustillant-add sugar, butter, sugar, glucose and butter and bring to boil then add cocoa powder and crushed almonds then put on a tray with parchment paper- be sure to spread to make a thin crunchy then bake at 335F fro 15 minutes-(recipe of Pierre Herme)

Notes

Add a little flour on the table and roll out the dough- not too thin- dock the dough- put in fridge for five minutes- then preheat oven and bake at 330F

Remove tart from tray after it is baked- chablonnage (brush chocolate on the inside of the tart) with dark chocolate
Ganash goes inside baked tart then allow to cool
