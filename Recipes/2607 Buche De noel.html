26/07 Buche De noel

Ingredients

1. ganache —


300g chocolat noir

300g creme


2. syrop—

250 eau

120g sucre

20g gran marnier or kuirch

vanilla or coffee or rhum


biscuitr cuillet

5 yolks/ jeunes

5 blanc whites

130g sucre

130g farine


4. butter cream


4 yolks

120g sucre

240g butter

90g dark chocolate

Directions

1. Ganache

Heat cream just before it begins to boil and add to chocolate cover with plastic wrap and place in the fridge

2. Syrop

Boil water and sugar and add it to the container with the grand Marnier

3. Bicuit cuiller

Separate eggs whites and whip the egg whites until you form many bubbles then add half sugar when it reaches soft peaks add the rest of the sugar whip until firm peaks. Fold in egg yolks don’t need to be full blended then add flour in two halves
Place in a pan with parchment paper spray pan before placing paper to ensure it stick. Also add a little spray to the paper. Spread with a large spatula to reduce the loss of bubbles. Bake at 370F
Place in oven for six minute and check. Cannot be really dark or ot will break.
Chocolate butter cream
Melt the dark chocolate in a bane au Marie add some butter to the chocolate to make it thinner to incorporate in the butter cream
Whisk your egg yolks to blanchair add the melted sugar adding it slowly (121c)when it cools to room temp or body temp depending on the temp of the butter, then begin adding the butter chunk by chunk slowly (pate a bomme). After incorporating all the gutter whip in the melted chocolate

Leave it room temp until use so it doesn’t set if you have to refrigerate you will need to whip again


Notes

Assembly
Remove the biscuit cuillière from the pan take paper off from bottom of cake- needs to be soaked turn upside down on a parchment paper then add the syrop after removing paper. Soak syrop into biscuit let it set the bottom part

Spread the ganche on the biscuit
Then roll the buiscuit let
After rolling use a grill to make it tighter hold the grill under the chest
Then put in the fridge
