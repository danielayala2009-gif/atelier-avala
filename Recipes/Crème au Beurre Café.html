Crème au Beurre Café

CRÈME

Ingredients

4 egg yolks

100g sugar

30ml water

215g butter

20g coffee extract

Directions

1. Heat sugar and water in a saucepan over medium heat [add a little bit more sugar to account for amount that won’t come out of saucepan] (size depends on amount of sugar, too big and most of syrup will stick to bottom of saucepan)
2. Whisk yolks - they need to be fluffy and pale
3. When sugar water gets near 121C there will be less bubbles and bigger. Make ice water, make fingers really cold, put syrup in ice water fast and see texture of ball. Looking for a malleable candy
4. Slowly pour syrup into side of yolks, while whisking yolks constantly. Can’t stop whisking until it is only warm - (body temperature)
5. Add butter in chucks with left hand, and whisk with right hand. Continue until all butter is incorporated. You don’t want chucks of butter in crème
6. Add coffee extract into butter cream and mix to incorporate.
