Génoise Chocolat

BISCUITS, PÂTISSERIE

Ingredients

4 eggs

2 egg yolks

150g flour

150g sugar

25g butter

30g Cocoa

Directions

1. Prepare mould with butter and flour so biscuit doesn’t stick
2. Whisk whole eggs and yolks until combined add sugar over double boiler. Heat up until sugar is melted. To check, dip finger in and rub to if it is smooth. Remove from heat once melted.
3. Whisk until becomes pale and fluffy with a lot of air bubbles.
4. Sift flour and cocoa powder into bowl. (Not all at once) Fold, repeat until all incorporated.
5. Sacrifice - Put 2tbsp of mixture with melted butter and mix. Then fold into mixture.
6. Pour into mould, on baking sheet, (not fill to top of mould) and bake immediately
6. Bake @ 350F -+ 15-20 minutes (set timer for 10 minutes to check)
