Gâteau Forêt Noire

ENTREMETS ET GÂTEAUX DE VOYAGE

Ingredients

Génoise

Génoise Chocolat


Syrup

200g sugar

200g water

QS vanilla

QS kirsch (cherry alcohol)


Crème Chantilly

600ml cream

60g sugar

2g vanilla


Garnish

60g dark chocolate

Fresh cherries

Description

German Cake made with cherries from the Black Forest
Invented in 1915 by Josef Keller

Directions

Génoise
Génoise Chocolat

Syrup
1. Bring water and sugar to boil in saucepan, until sugar is melted.
2. Pour on top of Kirsh and mix.

Crème Chantilly
1. Whisk cold cream and icing sugar until medium peaks. Put in fridge until ready to use.

Garnish
1. Melt chocolate over a double boiler.
2. On a cold piece of marble (not frozen) pour the chocolate and spread out using a large offset spatula into a thin layer. Leave to cool down and semi-set.
3.


Assembly
1. Un-mold the Génoise so it cools down quicker
2. Place acétate around clean mold.
3. Cut top on Génoise so it is flat, then cut into 3 even layers.
4. Chablonage the bottom of the bottom layer of Génoise. Refrigerate until it is set.
5. Place first piece of Génoise in mold and brush the syrup on until soaked.
6. Pipe the Chantilly with Tip-806 in spiral on top of layer
7. Place cherry halves on top of cream
8. Add second layer of Génoise and press a little bit to stick.
9. Brush the syrup on until soaked.
10. Pipe the Chantilly with Tip-806 in spiral on top of layer
11. Place cherry halves on top of cream
12. Add final layer of Génoise and press a little bit to stick.
13. Brush the syrup on until soaked
14. Remove mold and acétate
15. Pipe the Chantilly with Tip-806 in spiral on top of layer, then flatten with offset spatula
16. Add Chantilly to sides, making sure to cover so you can’t see Génoise.
17. Masquage: Using flat spatula, smooth sides so they are straight
18. Clean top, going from outside into the middle, cleaning spatula after each pass
19. Create rosettes on top of cake.
