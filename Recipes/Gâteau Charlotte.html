Gâteau Charlotte

ENTREMETS ET GÂTEAUX DE VOYAGE

Ingredients

Biscuit à la Cuillère ( Lady Fingers)

Crème Bavaroise

Directions

Biscuit à la Cuillère ( Lady Fingers)
Crème Bavaroise

Assembly
1. Cut the lady fingers so they are tight in the Mold
2. Cut your biscuits to be a bit smaller than the inner circle
3. Soak one biscuit with the syrup by brushing it on to the baking sheet side. Once you can push down on the biscuit a syrup appears around your finger, it is soaked.
4. Pour Crème Bavaroise until half full, spiral Coulis so every bite you get a bit of Coulis.
5. Soak the another biscuit, then push down a little bit into the crème.
6. Add a bit more creme, then Coulis, and finish with crème.
7. Freeze, then put a thin layer of Coulis on top.
