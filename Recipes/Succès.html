Décor
1. Melt sugar in pan on low heat to make dry caramel
2. Using wire, stab end of almond so you can dip in caramel when ready
3. Dip almond in caramel, once it has cooled a little bit, as we want to have a fine wire of caramel off the almond. Hang to set
4. Toast hazelnuts in oven

Assembly
1. Place ugliest dacquoise on the bottom, with spiral face down
2. Pipe butter cream in rosette using Tip-844, around perimeter of dacquoise. In the middle just fill in the space with butter cream
3. Add toasted hazelnuts on top of butter cream
4. Use paper to cover an area of the top of the pretty dacquoise, and cover the rest with icing sugar. Pipe a spiral rosette on top of the non-sugared part of the cake, after you place it on top of the butter cream.
