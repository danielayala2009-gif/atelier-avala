Macaron Au Chocolat (63)

Ingredients

200g sucre

117 almond powder

8 g cocoa powder

113g egg whites

50g sugar


Moyen age cathrine de zmedeci; macaroni

Italie 1533 France

XIX siecle macaron parisien (confitur/CB)

Maison Dalloyou et Maidon Ladurée

meringue francaise

meringue Italian

Directions

-Whip your egg whites after they have many bubbles add half the sugar whip to soft peaks then add the remaining half after whipping add the food coloring-gel based then add you. Make sure to whip until you get a birds peak.

-Sift combined almond powder, powdered sugar add cocoa powder then fold from the center out make sure to get the sides
Fill piping bag and with an 804 pipe pie your macarons
The macaron must sit to dry before cooking- they need to dry so when you cook the moisture in the egg will rise to give it volume- you do not have to so this with Italian merengue
Cook in 225F for 24 minutes

-At home you can raise the temp to 235 since the temperature in the oven fluctuates
