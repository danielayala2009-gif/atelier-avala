Crème Anglaise

CRÈME

Ingredients

250ml milk

3 egg yolks

62.5g sugar

Directions

1. Whisk yolks and sugar together just incorporate both together. Don’t over mix and pit too much air in mixture
2. Place milk in a large saucepan, and cook over medium heat
3. Once the first bubbles appear (if too hot will burn the yolks) place half the milk mixture onto eggs (tempering the eggs) and whisk to incorporate
4. Place back into saucepan (induction off) use whisk to incorporate then use spatula to stir (calmly, don’t want to add air to mixture)
5. Cook slowly (heat level 2) and continue until 80-82C
6. You will know it is ready when:
1. There is no longer foam (only if you are not stirring like a crazy person)
2. When stopping stirring the mixture will not continue to circle.
3. Cuisse à la nappe - when using a wooden spoon, you can pull it out of the mixture, and make a horizontal line and the top will not run into the bottom
7. Remove from saucepan once finished to stop cooking (To cool down fast, use the inverse of a double boiler with ice bath “double cooler”)
8. When covering crème anglaise, use plastic wrap and make sure that it makes contact with the surface of the crème anglaise
9. 3 days maximum in fridge (Can’t be frozen)
