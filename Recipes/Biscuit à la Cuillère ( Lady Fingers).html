Biscuit à la Cuillère ( Lady Fingers)

BISCUITS

Directions

1. Separate eggs
2. Prepare piping bag with Tip-806 and baking sheet with guides to keep all the same size (size = depth of mould + finger width)
3. Start whisking Eggs whites, once really foamy add half sugar (once started can’t stop or eggs will separate and deflate)
4. When it becomes really shiny add remaining sugar and whisk until stiff peaks are formed
5. Fold in egg yolks with spatula. Once half folded in add flour and continue to fold - this needs to be done quickly but gently or you will deflate the mixture.
6. Pipe with little angle, not verticale. Stop pressing at end, and then flick back. (If making Charlotte, can pipe fingers touching)
7. Perler- adding icing sugar on top, twice. Wait 15-30 seconds between times. Will look like perils after cooking
8. Bake @ 370F -+10 minutes (set timer for 6 minutes to check) we don’t want it too dark
9. Will last 2-3 days maximum in airtight container. Will freeze well.
