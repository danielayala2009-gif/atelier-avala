Mousse noisette (1/2 recette)

Directions

1. Sablée noisette

Cream your butter (buerre pomade)
Then add brown sugar ensure you have no lumps in the sugar before mixing
incorporate your 1/4 egg (12.5g)
Then add flour hazelnut powder and baking powder and salt mix
Dump on plastic wrap press and allow it to rest for 45min

Flour the table and top of the sablée and roll out to 4ml cut with the ring

Bake at 350 for about 7 min GBD

2. Creme citron lemon curd

Lemon juice
2 Eggs
1 Eggs yolks
corn strach
butter

Mix eggs together with sugar and mix tight away after its well mixed add corn starch and incorporate well- ensure no corn starch is sticking to the side of the bowl.
Heat lemon juice-temper eggs then add to the lemon juice on stove and cook- boil and continue mixing until thick
Add butter mix with mixer and cool

3. Daquoise (this version of daquoise has only almond flour so it is not too heavy)

Prep

tips and bag
Spray pan
Make circles and reverse the paper onto pan
Combine flours - icing sugar and almond powder

Beat egg white to foam then add half sugar beat then add remaining egg whites to soft peaks

Add icing sugar and almond powder mix fold inside with the spatula

Pipe onto circle in the pans then sprinkle with hazel nut powder cook in oven at 370F

4. Praline feulletin (like crushed ice cream cones)
Double boiler to melt praline with chocolate together
Want praliné to be soft and chocolate to melt once melted add the feulletin mix

5. Mousse
In a double boiler heat hazelnut paste
Have molds ready

- cream anglaise( or pate bomb- egg yolks and sugar)

-Italian meringue

-Caramelize your hazel nuts

Start with your Italian merengue then your pate bombe
Heat sirop for merengue to 121c then beat egg whites
Combine syrop with egg whites and whisk until it cool s down

Error - (Pâte a bombe- Blanchaire your egg yolks ( until they turn a pale yellow color)
Add sugar and water to heat to 121c then add it to the egg yolks beat until cool then add butte)

Cream anglaise not pate a bombe- follow recipe for creme anglaise

Mix hazel but paste to the cream anglaise and add to the Italian merengue fold together with whisk
