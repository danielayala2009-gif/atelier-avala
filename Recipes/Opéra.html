ENTREMETS ET GÂTEAUX DE VOYAGE

Ingredients

Ganache

250g chocolate 70%

200ml cream


Syrup

250ml water

120g sugar

8ml coffee extract


Biscuit Joconde

105g almond powder

105g icing sugar

30g flour

3 eggs

3 egg whites

30g sugar

22g butter, melted


Butter Cream

Crème au Beurre Café


Chablonage

chocolate 70%


Glaze

200g glazing paste (Cocoa Barry Pâte à Glazer) if don’t have use Chocolate with 10% oil

40g oil

Description

Invented in 1955 at Maison Dalloyou by Cyriaque Gavillon
Though, Gaston Cemôtie claimed that he invented it in 1960, to celebrate the Opera

TPT = Same amount of almond powder and icing sugar

Directions

Ganache
1) Warm cream in saucepan to first bubble.
2) Pour on top of chocolate
3) Start stirring in middle, and only move out once fully incorporated
4) Make sure there isn’t cream or small chunks of chocolate remaining.
5) Plastic wrap in contact, NOT in fridge, leave at Room Temperature

Syrup
1) Bring water and sugar to boil
2) Once boiled pour on top of coffee extract, and mix

Joconde
1) Put eggs, flour, almond powder and icing sugar in one bowl and whisk. Whisk until it is fluffy and whiter.
2) Put the egg whites in another bowl and whisk until medium-hard peaks. Adding half sugar when pretty fluffy, and other half when almost finished
3) Fold egg/flour mixture into egg whites/sugar using spatula. DON’T over mix or biscuit will be over
4) Sacrifice one tablespoon of mixture and mix into butter. Then fold back into mixture
5) Use small tray, with parchment paper. Paper needs to be flat (use oil to stick it down) then spray oil on top of paper
6) Pour mixture on top of tray and spread using large cake spatula, go to corners first, then the sides. Make smooth as quick as possible. (DON’T drop tray)
7) Bake at 350F until Golden Brown Delicious (approx. 10 minutes)

Butter Cream
Crème au Beurre Café

Glaze
1) Melt together in Double Boiler (just before needed)

Assembly
1) Cut Joconde into 3 even pieces - middle piece top, then sides at bottom and middle of cake.
2) Assemble on opposite side of tray
3) Melt chocolate in double boiler, once melted, brush bottom side of 1st piece of Joconde with a thin layer of chocolate to make the Chablonage. Put in fridge to set chocolate.
4) Brush 1/3 syrup onto opposite side of Chablonage Joconde until soaked. Don’t want to see the white of the Biscuit
5) Add half of butter cream on top on soaked biscuit (wait until syrup has soaked in, otherwise the syrup will mix with butter cream). Spread using small cake spatula and make an even layer. Go to edge of biscuit.
6) Place 2nd piece of Joconde (edge with middle: middle with edge), and press softly to stick to butter cream
7) Brush Joconde with 1/3 syrup
8) Add all of the ganache. Spread using small cake spatula and make an even layer. Go to edge of biscuit.
9) Place last piece of Joconde and press softly to stick to ganache
10) Brush Joconde with 1/3 syrup
11) Add remaining butter cream on top on soaked biscuit (wait until syrup has soaked in, otherwise the syrup will mix with butter cream). Spread using small cake spatula and make an even layer. Go to edge of biscuit.
12) Refrigerated until set.
13) Remove l’Inès in butter cream, once really cold. Needs to be done fast so we don’t warm up cake (put back in fridge)
14) Using a tray with a wire tray placed on an angle (one end raised) Pour Glaze on top of cake. Place in fridge for a minute or two to set
15) Use hot water to warm up knife for cutting. Dry knife before cutting, re-heat and clean/dry between each cut
16) On small pieces draw a treble clef, on a 6 person cake write OPERA
Will last in fridge form up to 5 days
