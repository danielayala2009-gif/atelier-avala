Atelier Ayala

Ingredients

  • Pâte sucrée shell
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 500 ml whole milk (or 400 ml milk + 100 ml cream)
  • 90–100 g sugar
  • 35 g cornstarch
  • 15 g flour
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 2–3 peaches
  • Butter + sugar
  • 1½–2 Tbsp Grand Marnier
  • Orange zest

Method

  1. Partially blind-bake the pâte sucrée.
  2. Caramelize peaches with butter, sugar, orange zest and Grand Marnier.
  3. Temper yolks and cook the vanilla custard on the stove until very thick.
  4. Layer custard → peaches → custard → remaining peaches.
  5. Bake at 190°C / 375°F for about 40–50 minutes.
  6. Cool completely; refrigerate overnight.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs (v1 baseline)
  • 100 g sugar (v1 baseline)
  • 120 g flour (v1 baseline)
  • 100 g browned butter (v1 baseline)
  • Grand Marnier
  • Honey (v2)
  • Vanilla
  • Orange zest
  • Salt

Method

  1. Brown and cool the butter.
  2. Build the batter gently without excessive aeration.
  3. Flavor with vanilla, citrus and Grand Marnier; v2 incorporates honey.
  4. Refrigerate overnight.
  5. Bake cold batter in prepared madeleine molds until humped and deeply golden.

Lineage: v1.0 12-piece formulation; v1.1 no-leavener experiment; v2.0 24-piece Grand Marnier–Honey overnight version.

Ingredients

  • 480 g milk
  • 28 g butter
  • 1 vanilla bean
  • 200 g sugar
  • 95 g flour
  • 1.5 g salt
  • 100 g whole egg
  • 35–40 g yolks
  • Optional branch: 50–60 g dark rum

Method

  1. Prepare a smooth, low-aeration batter.
  2. Rest refrigerated 24–48 hours.
  3. Bake ~15 minutes at 245°C / 475°F.
  4. Reduce to 190°C / 375°F and bake 45–60 minutes, judging by deep caramelization.

Keep the imperfect batch in the experiment record: dramatic rise, overflow/collapse, pale shell, then corrective caramelization.

Ingredients

  • Pâte sucrée: ~150 g flour, 113 g butter, 40 g powdered sugar, 1 yolk
  • Frangipane: 113 g butter, 100 g sugar, 100 g almond flour, 2 eggs
  • Pears
  • ~3 cups water
  • ½ cup sugar
  • Orange peel
  • ½ vanilla bean
  • 1 Tbsp Grand Marnier
  • 1 Tbsp dark rum

Method

  1. Prepare and chill pâte sucrée.
  2. Poach pears gently with vanilla, orange and liqueurs; drain well.
  3. Prepare frangipane.
  4. Fill shell and arrange pears.
  5. Bake until almond filling is set and deeply golden.

Preserve the Ayala poaching variation rather than normalizing it to a generic Bourdaloue.
